# Feature Proposal: "First 2 Projects Pro" Launch Initiative

## Objective
To accelerate user adoption and demonstrate the full power of CabEngine, we propose a "Project-Based Trial" system. Instead of a time-limited trial, users will be granted full Pro access for their first two successfully completed projects.

## Core Concept
Every new user starts with **2 "Pro Project" Credits**. 
- Users can design, experiment, and use basic features for free.
- For their first two projects, all premium features (Advanced DXF Exports, Branded PDFs, Excel Reports, and Custom Materials) are fully unlocked.
- This ensures users see the full professional output of the software before they are required to subscribe.

---

## Defining "Project Completion"
A project is considered "Completed" (consuming one of the two free credits) when the user reaches the **Value Realization Point**.

### Recommended Point: Quotation Approval
The credit should be consumed only when the user clicks **"Mark as Approved (Convert to Invoice)"**.

### Why this point?
1. **Professional Intent**: Approving a quotation signals that the design is finished and the user is moving toward a real-world transaction.
2. **Safe Experimentation**: Users won't "waste" their free credits on testing, draft projects, or deleted designs. They only pay (or use a credit) for success.
3. **Conversion Path**: Once a user has successfully closed a deal using a CabEngine Invoice, they are significantly more likely to subscribe to continue that success.

---

## User Experience & Transparency
- **Dashboard Visibility**: A clear status indicator (e.g., "Trial: 2 of 2 Pro Credits Remaining") will be visible on the dashboard.
- **Premium Indicators**: Professional features will be marked as "Included in Trial" instead of being locked with a paywall.
- **Milestone Notification**: When a user is about to approve their first project, a subtle notification will remind them that this will use one of their free professional credits.

---

## Business Benefits
- **Zero Friction**: New users can immediately start working without entering credit card details.
- **Quality Leads**: By the time the trial is over, the user has already experienced two successful project completions, making the subscription an easy business decision.
- **Competitive Edge**: Unlike competitors with 14-day trials, our project-based model aligns with the actual workflow of a kitchen designer.
